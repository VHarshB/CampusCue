# CampusCue — 58-Second Cinematic Launch Film

**Working title:** “45 Minutes”  
**Promise:** *Make your gap count.*  
**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920, 24 or 30 fps, burned-in captions, target **58 seconds**.  
**Tone:** Human, warm, premium, quiet confidence. Not a tech advertisement full of animated dashboards. Film real ASU campus life.

## Visual language (premium and relatable)

- **Look:** Deep navy/near-black end slate, warm natural sunlit campus shots, restrained cool-blue typography. Keep phones and skin tones natural; avoid cheesy AI holograms.
- **Camera:** 1 wide establishing shot, 3–4 close inserts (watch/phone/laptop/feet), 2 medium acting shots, one hopeful human moment. Use shallow depth-of-field if possible; simple phones are fine.
- **Edit:** Start with slower 2–3-second shots and a slight sense of loneliness; accelerate after message send; end with one longer warm shot.
- **Typography:** One clean sans-serif, large enough for social feed. No tiny interface animations, no dense technical bullets.
- **Sound:** Real campus footsteps + backpack zipper + iMessage send; restrained music that opens up after the recommendation. Use licensed/royalty-free audio.
- **Actor direction:** Quiet “I have nothing to do” rather than exaggerated sadness. Ending is a small authentic hello, not an implausible instant best-friend montage.

## On-camera demo prompt (match QA test)

> “I have 45 minutes free. I'm near Hayden Library, feeling social, and want something free. What should I do before my next class?”

**Integrity/privacy:** This video can dramatize the student's feelings and campus visit. **It cannot fake the assistant output as live functionality.** Use permissioned actors, mask notifications and personal calendar details, and label any illustrative UI as concept footage if used at all. Do not imply live student matching or background GPS tracking.
